<h2>Research</h2>
<p style="text-align:justify;">
  My research focuses on the intersection of <strong>Machine Learning</strong>, <strong>Computer Vision</strong>, and <strong>Vision-Language Models</strong>.  
  I am particularly interested in the design of <strong>efficient multimodal architectures</strong> for zero-shot understanding,  
  cross-domain adaptation, and representation learning.  
  My work also explores <strong>vision-language integration</strong> and <strong>ensemble tuning of pretrained models</strong> for  
  robust scene understanding in complex environments.
</p>
