<!-- ================== Unit 2 ================== -->
<div style="background:#eef6ff; border-left:5px solid #007BFF; padding:20px; border-radius:8px; margin-bottom:30px;">
  <h3>Unit 2 – Empirical Research Framework</h3>

  <h4 style="margin-top:20px;">Learning Focus</h4>
  <ul>
    <li>Design reproducible experiments</li>
    <li>Understand internal, external, construct, and conclusion validity</li>
    <li>Develop research questions and hypotheses</li>
    <li>Translate theoretical ideas into measurable experimental variables</li>
  </ul>
